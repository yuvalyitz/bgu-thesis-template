{\noindent\Large\textbf{Abstract}}

\vspace{3ex}

This document is a template for a thesis at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, and at the same time its manual.
An English thesis and the Hebrew summary, abstract and cover that the university requires at the end of the bound volume are produced as one PDF whose links work in both directions: the English table of contents lists the Hebrew pages, the Hebrew table of contents shows the English chapter numbers and pages read from the English build, and Hebrew text can cite the bibliography and link to English theorems, figures and definitions while printing their numbers automatically.

The chapters explain the build process, the anchor and label conventions that connect the two parts, and the commands for writing Hebrew, and they exercise every construct a thesis needs along the way: numbered chapters and sections, a theorem stated in the introduction and restated with its proof, a definition, a figure, a table, an algorithm, code listings and citations.
