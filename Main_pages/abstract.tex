{\noindent\Large\textbf{Abstract}}

\vspace{3ex}

This document is a template for a thesis at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev.
It shows a working setup for an English thesis with the Hebrew summary and abstract that the university requires at the end of the bound volume, produced as one PDF whose links work in both directions.

Everything a real thesis needs is exercised at least once: numbered chapters and sections, theorems that are stated in the introduction and restated with their proofs later, definitions, an algorithm, a figure, a table, citations, and a table of contents that also lists the Hebrew pages.
The Hebrew part has its own table of contents whose numbers and page references are read automatically from the English build, and whose entries are clickable links into the English chapters.

Replace this abstract with your own.
