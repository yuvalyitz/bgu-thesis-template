This thesis showed that \textsc{Template Problem} is \NP-hard (\Cref{thm:hardness}) but fixed-parameter tractable in the number of machines (\Cref{thm:main}).
Two questions remain open.

\begin{openq}
Does \textsc{Template Problem} admit a polynomial kernel when parameterized by the number of machines?
\end{openq}

\begin{openq}
Is the running time of \Cref{thm:main} optimal under the Exponential Time Hypothesis?
\end{openq}
